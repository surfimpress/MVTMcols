Post-1980 cutter — held-out test on 1986 / 1998 / 2004

What this is. The post-1980 modular cutter (tools/post1980/) was developed and eyeballed entirely on the ten Phase-0 issues (1985, 1990, 1995, 2000, 2007 — Feb & Oct). This page is the first run on issues that were never used during development: three fresh issues, one early (1986, the low-resolution A4-rewrap generation), one mid (1998 broadsheet), one late (2004 broadsheet), each at page 1 (front), page 3 (interior editorial) and page 10 (deep interior / ads & classifieds). Nine pages, no parameter touched.

How to read it. Left = original scan, right = the cutter's detections overlaid. Open each image full-size to zoom — the captions below describe gross structure (does each coloured box land on a real reading unit?). I have not verified fine column-grid accuracy at full resolution; that is exactly what the right-hand images let you check by eye. Captions are deliberately honest about misses, not just hits.

Key — overlay colours
Article — blue box around an article body; darker-blue inner box = detected headline.
Display ad — red box.
Photo — light-pink box.
Uncovered — orange box: visible ink not claimed by any detector (a miss flagged for review).
Masthead / section banner — grey fill across the top.
Structural grid — racing-green dashed: column gutters, whitespace bands, text-area edges, masthead bottom.

2004-09-21  p10 — deep interior (classifieds) [modular · 1 article · 5 ads · 4 photos]

Gap worth flagging: this is a classifieds page ("SOCIAL NOTES" banner over dense narrow-column tiny-type listings), but it was classified as generic modular, not as a distinct classifieds page-class — so the listing columns aren't handled with a classifieds-specific grid. The right-hand display ads are caught, but the classifieds body is the known unhandled case the Phase-0 notes anticipated.

Overall (honest read). On three issues never seen during development, the cutter holds up at the structural level across all three eras: mastheads/section banners, article-as-rectangle blocks, multi-column headlines, and display ads are detected sensibly — a different universe from the "beyond useless" classical cuts on 1980. Two recurring gaps stand out: (1) borderless photos on the 1986 low-res scans fall through to "uncovered" orange instead of being classified, and (2) classifieds pages aren't yet a distinct page-class (2004 p10). News-brief→ad misclassification on dense interior bottoms (1986 p3) is a third, smaller one. None of these are grid-paradigm failures; they're specific detector gaps. Fine column-grid accuracy still needs a full-res eyeball — which these overlays support.
